# Thrown Objects — Wound Tables

| Heft (added Impact) | Examples | Strength needed to throw it |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Empty can or bottle, apple, pebble, shoe | Any |
| +1 | Fist-sized rock, full bottle, hammer, wrench | Any |
| +2 | Brick, rock the size of a head, pipe wrench, hatchet thrown end over end | 30 or more |
| +3 | Cinder block, small boulder, car battery; two hands, only at a target within reach of a step | 50 or more |
| Too heavy | Toolbox, sack of cement, tire on its rim, anything heavier | Cannot be thrown; it can only be dropped or shoved |

| Locations | Minor | Serious | Severe | Catastrophic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head, neck — vital | 1 | 2–4 | 5 | 6+ |
| Torso, waist | 1–2 | 3–5 | 6 | 7+ |
| Groin and limbs — cap Severe | 1–2 | 3–5 | 6+ | — |

## Head — vital

| Impact | Severity / Trauma | Version | Wound | Justification |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor / 0 | Agreed | **Ducked into it.** Lose the next Movement Stage. | — |
| 2 | Serious / +1 | Agreed | **Rock to the head.** Minor Concussion. Body Save. Success: only Dazed, and 0 Trauma instead of +1. Failure: Dazed and `-10 Handle` until 1 Long Rest. | — |
| 3 | Serious / +1 | Agreed | **Struck in the face.** Dazed. `-20 Handle` on ranged and visually precise actions until 1 Long Rest. After 1 Long Rest: lasting `-10 Handle` on ranged and visually precise actions. | — |
| 4 | Serious / +1 | Agreed | **Broken nose or cheekbone.** Lose the next Action Stage. Visually precise actions at Disadvantage until 1 Long Rest. After 1 Long Rest: lasting `-10 Charm` at GM discretion. | — |
| 5 | Severe / +2 | Agreed | **Severe Concussion.** Body Save. Success: Dazed and `-10 Handle` until 1 Long Rest; +1 Trauma instead of +2. Failure: Dazed and `-30 Handle`. After 1 Long Rest: `-10 Handle`. After 2 Long Rests: the Handle penalty clears; lasting `-10 Intellect`. | — |
| 6+ | Catastrophic / +4 | Agreed | **Skull fracture.** Immediate Death Save. Knocked down. Dazed for 1d4 turns. Physical Checks at Disadvantage until 2 Long Rests. After 2 Long Rests: lasting `-20 Body`, `-10 Intellect` and `+4 minimum Stress`. | — |
