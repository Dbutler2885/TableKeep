# Piercing — Wound Tables

## Scope and bands

| Locations | Minor | Serious | Severe | Critical | Catastrophic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neck, torso, waist — vital | 1–2 | 3–4 | 5–6 | 7–8 | 9+ |
| Head, groin | 1–3 | 4–5 | 6–7 | 8–9 | 10+ |
| Upper arm, thigh — cap Critical | 1–3 | 4–5 | 6–7 | 8+ | — |
| Forearm, hand, shin, feet — cap Severe | 1–3 | 4–5 | 6+ | — | — |
