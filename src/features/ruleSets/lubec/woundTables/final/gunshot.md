# Gunshot — Wound Tables

| Locations | Minor | Serious | Severe | Critical | Catastrophic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Head, neck — vital | — | 1–2 | 3 | 4 | 5+ |
| Torso, waist, groin | 1 | 2–3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
| Upper arm, thigh — cap Critical | 1 | 2–3 | 4 | 5+ | — |
| Forearm, hand, shin, feet — cap Severe | 1 | 2–3 | 4+ | — | — |

## Head — vital

| Impact | Severity / Trauma | Version | Wound | Justification |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Serious / +1 | Unchanged | **Bullet glances off the skull.** Minor Concussion. Body Save. Success: only Dazed, and 0 Trauma instead of +1. Failure: Minor Concussion, which is Dazed and `-10 Handle` until 1 Long Rest. | Retains the established glancing-hit outcome. |
| 2 | Serious / +1 | Agreed | **Face, jaw or ear wound.** Lose the next Action Stage. Speech badly impaired until 1 Long Rest. `-20 Handle` on ranged and visually precise actions until 1 Long Rest. After 1 Long Rest: *Facial injury*, lasting `-10 Charm` at GM discretion. | — |
| 3 | Severe / +2 | Agreed | **Skull hit.** Severe Concussion. Body Save. Success: Minor Concussion—Dazed, with `-10 Handle` and `-10 Intellect` until 1 Long Rest; +1 Trauma instead of +2. Failure: Dazed and `-30 Handle`. After 1 Long Rest: `-10 Handle`. After 2 Long Rests: the Handle penalty clears; *Persistent concussion*, lasting `-10 Intellect`. | — |
| 4 | Critical / +2 | Agreed | **Bullet in the skull.** Apply the Skull hit concussion effects at Impact 3, including Dazed, its concussion Body Save, penalties and recovery. Retain +2 Trauma regardless of the concussion Save. Death Save in d4 turns unless a separate Body Save succeeds. If survived, lasting: `-10 Intellect`, `-10 Body`, and `+4 minimum Stress`. | — |
| 5+ | Catastrophic / +4 | Agreed | **Brain.** Immediate Death Save. If survived: apply the Skull hit concussion effects at Impact 3, including Dazed, its concussion Body Save, penalties and recovery. Retain +4 Trauma regardless of the concussion Save. If survived, lasting: `-20 Intellect`, `-20 Body`, and `+8 minimum Stress`. | — |
